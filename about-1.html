About QuickQuote Pro

QuickQuote Pro was built for people who do real work — plumbers, electricians, drivers, cleaners, freelancers and small business owners — not accountants sitting behind a desk. Most invoicing software assumes you have time to learn a dashboard. We assume you're standing in a customer's driveway and need a professional invoice in the next sixty seconds.

The tool offers three focused documents: an Invoice Generator for billing completed work, a Quote Generator for sending price estimates before a job starts, and a Service Request tool for customers who need to describe a job to a professional. Each one works instantly on any phone, with no signup required.

We support 55+ professions and 35+ currencies, so whether you're a contractor in Texas, an electrician in Dubai, or a freelance designer in Manila, the invoice you send looks professional in your local currency and your trade.

An optional free account lets you save your invoice history and access it from any device — but you never need one just to create and send a document.
