Frequently Asked Questions

Is QuickQuote Pro free?

Yes, completely free with no signup required. An optional free account adds invoice history across devices.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Every tool works fully as a guest. Accounts are only needed if you want to save and revisit your document history.

What's the difference between Invoice, Quote and Service Request?

An Invoice bills for completed work and includes payment status. A Quote is a price estimate sent before work starts. A Service Request lets a customer describe a job to a professional, with no payment details at all.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes, the entire site is mobile-first and can be installed as an app on your home screen.

How many professions and currencies are supported?

55+ professions and 35+ currencies covering the Americas, Europe, the Gulf, Africa and Asia.
