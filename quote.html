Send Price Estimates Customers Trust

A quote sets expectations before you pick up a tool or open your laptop. It protects both sides: your customer knows roughly what they'll pay, and you have something in writing if the scope changes later. QuickQuote Pro's quote generator lets you build a clean, professional estimate in the same amount of time it takes to send a text message.

Every quote includes a Valid Until date, so customers understand your pricing won't hold forever, and a Terms & Conditions field for anything you need to spell out — deposits, material costs, or cancellation policy. Once the customer accepts and the job is finished, tap "Convert Quote into Invoice" and the customer name, pricing and description carry straight over — no retyping.

Why Send a Quote Before Starting Work

Jobs that start without an agreed price are the most common source of payment disputes. A customer who never saw a number in writing feels entitled to negotiate after the work is done, when you have the least leverage. A quote flips that: the price is agreed before you show up, so the conversation at the end is about payment, not negotiation. It also filters out customers who were never going to accept your rate, saving you a wasted trip.

What Makes a Strong Quote

  • A specific description — "fix the sink" invites scope creep; "replace kitchen sink trap and re-seal connection" doesn't.
  • A validity date — material costs change; a quote from three months ago shouldn't be binding today.
  • Clear terms — deposit requirements, what happens if the scope changes, and who supplies materials.
  • A professional look — the same visual quality as your invoices builds trust before the job even starts.

How to Send a Quote

  1. Enter the customer's name and select your profession and currency.
  2. Describe the work and enter your estimated amount, tax and discount.
  3. Set how long the quote is valid and add any terms.
  4. Download it or tap "Save & Get Shareable Link" to send it over WhatsApp.

From Quote to Invoice, Automatically

The biggest friction in most quoting workflows is re-entering the same information twice — once for the quote, again for the invoice once the job is approved. QuickQuote Pro removes that step entirely. Tap "Convert Quote into Invoice" on any quote you've created, and it opens the Invoice Generator with the customer name, profession, currency, description, amount, tax and discount already filled in. You just confirm the details, set the payment method and status, and send.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a quote and an invoice?

A quote is a price estimate you send before work begins, so a customer knows what to expect. An invoice is issued after the work is done and requests payment.

Can I turn a quote into an invoice later?

Yes. Once a customer accepts your quote and the work is complete, tap "Convert Quote into Invoice" to carry over the customer, pricing and description into a new invoice automatically.

Can I set an expiry date on my quote?

Yes, every quote includes a "Valid Until" date so customers know how long the pricing is guaranteed.
