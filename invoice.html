The Free Invoice Generator Built for Real Work

Most invoice software is built for accountants sitting at a desk. QuickQuote Pro is built for the person actually doing the job — the plumber finishing a callout, the electrician wrapping up a rewiring job, the freelance designer closing out a project, or the driver who just completed a delivery run. You need to hand over a professional invoice on the spot, from your phone, without creating an account or learning new software.

This invoice generator supports 55+ professions — from plumbers, electricians and HVAC technicians to drivers, IT support specialists, tutors and business consultants — and 35+ currencies spanning the US Dollar, Euro, British Pound, UAE Dirham, Saudi Riyal, Indian Rupee, Nigerian Naira, Philippine Peso and more. Whichever trade you're in and wherever your customers are, the invoice you generate looks professional and gets you paid faster.

How to Create an Invoice in 4 Steps

  1. Fill in your business name, logo and the customer's details.
  2. Choose your profession and currency, then describe the work and the amount.
  3. Set the tax, discount, payment method and payment status.
  4. Tap "Save & Get Shareable Link" or download it as a PDF/JPG and send it directly over WhatsApp.

Invoice vs. Quote vs. Service Request

Document Used When Includes Payment?
InvoiceWork is done and payment is dueYes
QuoteEstimating a price before work startsNo — estimate only
Service RequestA customer is asking for a serviceNo

If you already sent a quote and the customer accepted, you can convert it into an invoice with one tap once the work is complete.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes. Creating, downloading and sharing invoices is completely free with no signup required. An optional free account lets you save your invoice history across devices.

Can I use this invoice maker on my phone?

Yes, the tool is fully mobile-friendly and works directly in your phone's browser. You can also install it as an app on your home screen.

Which currencies and professions are supported?

Over 35 currencies covering the US, Europe, the Gulf, Africa and Asia, and 55+ professions from plumbers and electricians to drivers, IT support and business consultants.

Can my customer see the invoice after I send it?

Yes. When you tap "Save & Get Shareable Link", a unique link is created. Anyone who opens that link sees the exact same invoice, including any status updates you make later.
