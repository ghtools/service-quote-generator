Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What We Collect

You can use QuickQuote Pro's Invoice, Quote and Service Request tools without creating an account. If you choose to create a free account, we store your email address and the documents you save, so you can access them from any device.

Shared Links

When you tap "Save & Get Shareable Link", the document (customer name, amounts, description, etc.) is stored and made accessible to anyone who has that specific link. Do not share a document link containing sensitive information with people you don't want to see it.

Third-Party Services

We use Supabase for account authentication and data storage. We do not sell your data to advertisers or third parties.

Your Choices

You can delete your saved documents or your account at any time by contacting us at support@quickquotepro.app.
